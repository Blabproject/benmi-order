# LINE Pay checkout logo

`/line-pay-logo.png` is the unmodified `LINE Pay_PNG/LINE-Pay(h)_W238_n.png`
from LINE Pay's official PNG download:

- Guidelines: https://developers-pay.line.me/logo-guideline
- Download: https://developers-pay.line.me/assets/files/logo_png-dde164bdd7f9c679c633d58c17757e61.zip
- Retrieved: 2026-11-03

The logo remains LINE Pay's trademark, not an open-source asset. Their guidelines
restrict use to merchants and partners with a LINE Pay contract. Checkout displays
it only when the tenant's LINE Pay payment method is enabled.

Keep the original colors and aspect ratio. The horizontal logo is displayed at
24px high (above the official 20px minimum), with 12px of clear space on every
side. The adjacent visible LINE Pay text labels the radio button; the image is
decorative for assistive technology.
